What DC FAT is

DC FAT is the native currency of Datachain Rope. It is not a DCR-20 contract. New FAT is created by knot-reward emission (genesis 10 billion, asymptotic maximum about 18 billion, 4-year halving), not by minting a wrap.

WFAT (0x285eecf51d5f0a6ab8d8151139b4d19b05c6b3e4) is the 1:1 DCR-20 wrap used by DCSwap. Price policy on dcscan is WFAT price = FAT price, taken from the DCSwap canonical feed.

Circulating supply on this page is Scenario A from /supply (excludes labelled uncirculated wallets). It is not WFAT totalSupply and not the 10B genesis figure.
