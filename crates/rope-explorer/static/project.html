        Connected wallet matches this project's owner. Edit campaign copy, publish to open voting, archive a closed campaign, or resubmit after archive/rejection.

        How does voting work?
        Holders connect a wallet and cast a signed ballot. Weight sums legacy DC on Ethereum, legacy DC on XDC, native FAT on Datachain Rope, and DCR-20 WFAT on Rope. Approval needs more than half the weight cast and at least 1,000,000 FAT of total participation. The 1,000,000 FAT figure is the quorum floor for the whole campaign, not a per-wallet lock amount.
        Does voting lock all FAT in my wallet?
        No. Your discover ballot is a signed off-chain record: the full cross-chain balance is used as weight, but tokens stay in your wallet unless you also cast into the on-chain VoteEscrow for this campaign. When you do use VoteEscrow with Return disposition, only the amount you escrow is locked for the window and released to that same wallet at close - not your entire balance, and not a flat 1M FAT lock.
        What chains count for the governance pool?
        Members can register DCR-20 wallets on Datachain Rope (DC FAT / FAT / WFAT), ERC-20/ERC-777 DC on Ethereum, and XRC-20 DC on XDC Network via Join Governance Pool on the Vote page.
        Is this financial advice or a securities offering?
        No. Community Vote decides which projects may build on Datachain Rope infrastructure. It is not an investment solicitation.
        Where is the audit trail?
        Submissions, reviews, and ballots are anchored as knots on the governance ledger and visible via DCScan / rope RPC.
        Can I propose an NGO for the residual DC allocation?
        Yes, when nominations are open on a campaign page. Connect a wallet, fill in the organisation details, and sign the nomination. Nominations build the candidate shortlist; the campaign ballot itself decides community authority over the residual reserve, not the final beneficiary.
