Register Your Databox

Operating a rope deploy node (databox / rpc-slot / witness / community-node) or an Ecosystem Deployment Console node? Register it here - no admin approval needed. Registration is signed with your operator wallet (EIP-191, domain DCROPE-DATABOX-AUTH) and anchored on-chain for a durable, auditable record. Your node must then call POST /api/v1/databoxes/<id>/heartbeat periodically (every < 10 minutes) to stay marked online.
