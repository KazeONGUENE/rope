    How does voting work?
    DC FAT holders connect a wallet on Datachain Rope and cast a signed ballot. Voting weight equals their live native DC FAT balance. Projects need more than 50% approval weight and at least 1,000,000 FAT of total participation.
    What chains count for the governance pool?
    Members can register DCR-20 wallets on Datachain Rope (DC FAT / FAT / WFAT), ERC-20/ERC-777 DC on Ethereum, and XRC-20 DC on XDC Network via Join Governance Pool on the Vote page.
    Is this financial advice or a securities offering?
    No. Community Vote decides which projects may build on Datachain Rope infrastructure. It is not an investment solicitation.
    Where is the audit trail?
    Submissions, reviews, and ballots are anchored as knots on the governance ledger and visible via DCScan / rope RPC.
